## Positioning

WalTrade is an independent R&D project building an Autonomous Capital
Management Platform.

## Public communication principles

- Evidence before narrative.
- Public communication consumes canonical evidence; it never creates project
  evidence or authority.
- No performance promises.
- No investment solicitation.
- No trading signals.
- No proprietary strategy thresholds or mechanics.
- No cherry-picking positive outcomes.
- Failures, WAIT states, rejected hypotheses, and negative evidence may be
  communicated.
- Any performance evidence must clearly distinguish PAPER from LIVE and retain
  appropriate context.
- AI must be described as AI-assisted research/engineering; financial authority
  remains evidence-driven and human-governed.
- No investment product is currently offered.
- Public communication does not change the Roadmap, experiment gates, or LIVE
  authority.
